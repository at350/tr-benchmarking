# CORE GUARDRAILS

STATUS: ACTIVE

Purpose
Centralize the shared non-substantive guardrails used across Frank, Karthic, and Dasha files.

Rules
- Do not invent facts, writings, signatures, party roles, citations, or jurisdiction-specific rules.
- Do not use generic IRAC when the workflow requires gate-by-gate analysis.
- Distinguish enforceability or formality from proof difficulty.
- Keep the controlling gate before fallback doctrines, substitutes, or defenses.
- Keep independent barriers separate.
- Keep metadata separate from scoring.
- Prefer full doctrine names over abbreviations when outputs will be compared or clustered.
- Keep non-triggered secondary issues brief.
- State specific uncertainty; do not rely on empty “it depends” language.
- Do not reward polish, verbosity, or formatting as a substitute for legal reasoning.
